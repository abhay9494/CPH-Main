# Design System

## Theme
- **Global Theme**: Dark Mode (Stealth oriented)

## Color Palette
Strict adherence to the following HEX colors:
- **Primary Accent**: \`#4285f4\` (Google Blue - used for primary actions, active states)
- **Success/Safe**: \`#00cc66\` (Green - used for positive confirmations, active stealth status)
- **Danger/Alert**: \`#f14c4c\` (Red - used for warnings, errors, stopping processes)

## Typography & UI/UX
- **Font Family**: 'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif.
- **Font Sizing**: 
  - Base: 13px/14px for readability without taking up too much space.
  - Headers: 16px/18px bold.
- **Layout Elements**:
  - Transparent/Glassmorphic backgrounds for overlays.
  - Custom scrollbars (8px width, dark thumb).
  - Non-selectable text globally (\`user-select: none\`) to prevent accidental highlighting during use.
  - Smooth transitions (0.3s ease) for opacities and hovers to maintain a premium feel.
